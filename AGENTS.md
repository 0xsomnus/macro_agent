# Instructions for coding agents

## Read before implementation

Read `README.md`, `docs/PRODUCT_DOCTRINE.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, then the relevant domain and architecture documents and `docs/ADR/` records. [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) indexes the reference material. Keep the root README oriented toward human setup, usage and honest current status. Treat settled principles as invariants. Treat open decisions as questions for joint design, not defaults to hide in code. The user prefers focused Markdown handoffs, concise honest feedback, Socratic architectural discussion, and no em dash in generated documents.

## Working rules

1. Build the smallest vertical slice that proves timely monitoring and useful thesis challenge. Use interfaces and recorded fixtures before broad connector coverage.
2. Preserve exact approved `UserThesis` text. Keep compiled interpretation, factual conflicts, assessments, and system knowledge separate. Only explicit user approval changes the current thesis version.
3. Keep event capture and deterministic routing/urgency controls independent of LLM availability. Broad triage may use models, but uncertainty/failure cannot silently become non-material. Investigate credible severe surprises outside mapped drivers; novelty alone is insufficient. Search only for a named unresolved material question and log cost/trigger.
4. Model provenance, known-at time, source revision, licensing status, and replay from the start. Do not make claims of full coverage based on one API call.
5. Keep causal edges distinct from correlations. Version edges and regimes; route canonical changes through review even when thresholds fire.
6. Define bounded execution graphs with explicit node schemas, budgets, failure paths, and authority gates. Specialists are invoked on demand.
7. For valuation, choose method by business type and run arithmetic in deterministic code. Record every assumption and sensitivity. Decline unsupported methods rather than forcing a DCF.
8. Follow the accepted Django/PostgreSQL persistence foundation. Keep provider, LLM, queue, and deployment choices replaceable until reviewed. Do not assume OpenBB or a terminal subscription grants redistribution or model-processing rights.
9. Add meaningful tests for invariants and replay, not tests that merely mirror implementation. Compare reasoning with deterministic baseline in evaluation work.
10. Report confirmed facts, assumptions, coverage limits, and remaining decisions separately. Ask for architectural choices with concrete options and tradeoffs.
11. Rough headline-led ideas are expected inputs. Propose and explain evidence-qualified omitted drivers before consequential clarification; do not require the trader to complete the research framework. Keep explanation validity, thesis progression and prospective entry attractiveness separate, without inventing timing, targets, stops or R:R. Approval covers exact text and a versioned concise interpretation. Material driver/horizon/signpost changes require user approval; agent hypotheses remain separate. Refine rough theses by exposing gaps and assumptions, not by manufacturing conviction.
12. Continuous news monitoring, morning briefs, counter-analysis, attached-trade relevance, and cumulative evidence are core. Preserve global context around the narrowed paper-pilot instruments. Research is not restricted to those instruments; exploratory trade-idea research does not request monitoring or initiate a coverage-review workflow. Scheduled-source fixtures alone cannot establish desk coverage.
13. Distinguish public availability, actual system receipt, and durable known-at time. Pin interpretation, exposure, source, macro-context, rule, and model dependencies. Save notification intent with assessment atomically; stale analysis cannot replace a newer current revision.
14. Defer the entire automated outcome-learning chain from the pilot, including provisional retrieval. Keep basic history/outcomes, current evidence updates, and named-human knowledge correction review. Internal configured BYOK is allowed; external BYOK, adaptive routing, and subscription allocation are deferred/open as documented.
15. Follow ADR 014: Python owns API/domain/research workers; TypeScript owns the web UI. Keep domain and publication rules independent of frameworks. Frontend/client tooling, queue, hosting, and model providers still require review; the disposable test harness is not a production architecture.
16. Follow ADR 015: audit small domain modules, force correction/publication orderings through independent connections, and expose read-only transition evidence. All governing-state writers share the publication ordering protocol. Sample the trusted clock after acquiring protection. Preserve original decisions separately from current disposition, including retries. Laboratory database behavior does not prove another adapter or external delivery.
17. Follow ADR 016: use Django with PostgreSQL, Django ORM/migrations, authentication, sessions, and restricted internal admin. Target Django 5.2 LTS and Python 3.13 with the psycopg driver. Keep immutable records read-only in admin and route workflow actions through explicit application services. Built-in auth does not establish private-row isolation.
18. Follow ADR 017, accepted through delegated judgment on 2026-10-05: use explicit DRF serializers and APIViews with drf-spectacular OpenAPI generated from their definitions. Reject duplicate JSON keys, unknown fields, and coercion; preserve exact text. Derive the actor from the session, enforce owner scope in every service, require CSRF on session writes, and bind approval to exact hashes with a protected current-version comparison. Historical retries cannot reactivate stale state. Approval time is not proof of exact durable known-at time. Do not add Ninja or Pydantic as competing wire authorities.
19. Preserve the [paper-position contracts](docs/PAPER_POSITIONS.md): exact manual declarations, explicit gaps, reviewed-approval provenance and terminal closure. Bound publication and all governing writers protect owner, thesis and affected briefs in consistent order before sampling time. Approval/exposure changes atomically invalidate current briefs and pending notices; pending context blocks publication until committed-input admission. Conservative input observation does not measure exact admission commit time or establish full operational replay. Keep the complete exposure book within the documented internal record bound; never silently truncate it.
20. The internal user-input CLI uses the existing session/CSRF HTTP API. Recorded-news examples require explicit local settings, a development/test database and the synthetic setup gate. Preserve exact approval preview and manual interpretation labels; fixture screening is not personalized analysis. Keep live monitoring, source/provider rights and analytical readiness separate from the recorded workflow checkpoint.
21. Internal compilation uses a provider-independent catalogue and explicitly selected provider/model. Persist admission before inference, release database locks during calls, then compare protected thesis state before proposing an immutable interpretation. Preserve model provenance, grounded quotations, separate questions/hypotheses, recorded limits and unknown costs. Exact quotations establish attribution, not semantic fidelity or factual verification. Never silently retry paid or uncertain attempts, invent missing intent, or activate model output without explicit approval. The text-only checkpoint has no current macro context or verified facts.

22. Follow the [daily-publication contract](docs/DAILY_PUBLICATION.md): separate typed daily admission, exact retained digest, one immutable decision per review, protected current pointer and atomic internal notification intent. Sources precede sorted owners/theses/briefs; all governing writers invalidate current authority and pending notices atomically. Preflight complete source fanout before activation and enforce it again on correction/permission changes. Historical retry cannot reactivate state; null current reads cannot fall back to history. Daily intents have no external delivery or acknowledgement authority.

23. Assess explanatory support separately from observed price direction. Surface material changes to central drivers promptly even if direction remains intact or no trade is attached; the separate qualified internal route is implemented, while external delivery and live materiality calibration remain open. Follow the [driver-review requirement](docs/DRIVER_REVIEW.md), preserve unresolved attribution, and keep trader-observed prices separate from verified market data. Retained research evidence can precede thesis approval. Keep selected source claims separate from quoted trader intent; conflicts remain model-identified and answerable through retained refinement questions. Source-backed compilation/approval protects sources before owner/thesis and rejects changed dependencies without editing approved text. Fresh daily synthesis preserves the exact retained review and complete paper book, commits admission before inference and shares private-call limits. It adds no publication/notification authority. Runner synthesis requires explicit pinned opt-in and a protected live lease at admission; recovery never resends an admitted uncertain call. Current synthesis reads stay within the exact protected published review, with no historical fallback. See [source-backed research](docs/SOURCED_RESEARCH.md) and [morning synthesis](docs/MORNING_SYNTHESIS.md).

24. Follow [ADR 019](docs/ADR/019-question-research-and-driver-publication.md). Question-led research has at most two admitted model calls and three named metadata questions; omitted source recovery preserves legacy one-shot searches. Explicit recovery pins at most three source-only attempts per question, shared pacing/cooldown and a source deadline. Retain original failures, exact capture bindings and successful searches; honor longer server delays, fence expired dispatch and stop before paid planning when a known cooldown makes acquisition impossible. See [source recovery](docs/RESEARCH_SOURCE_RECOVERY.md). Retain exact input, counter-evidence questions, candidate exclusions, source revisions and unknown costs. New research packets carry the complete retained plan/query outcomes into synthesis as separate untrusted workflow context, including empty searches and selection limits. Pin and protect every consumed query source, even when it returned no selected headline. Preserve legacy packet/prompt identities. Workflow context cannot supply trader intent, factual quotations or proof of absent counter-evidence. Private research queries require owner-scoped source eligibility and membership. Research does not enroll monitoring. Saved command retries read only; explicit continuation cannot resend an admitted uncertain paid node. Alternative sources require an explicit configured contract and are not silently substituted. Qualified news publication is separate from model recommendation, requires retained cumulative driver context and protected current dependencies, and fences automatic admission with the exact live watch/slot. Carry unread work with its direct original request and complete current original evidence. New-wording suppression is not proof of non-materiality. No external send or acknowledgement authority is added.

The optional driver-review profiles require typed evidence-linked proposals and complete exact-driver reassessment. Preserve legacy schema/hash identities when omitted. Price observation remains unavailable and entry not assessed in these profiles; analytical attention recommendations carry no publication or notification authority. See [driver review](docs/DRIVER_REVIEW.md).

25. Follow [background research and search accounting](docs/RESEARCH_BACKGROUND.md). Worker enrollment is separate owner consent for an already-planned recovery run, with a pinned model configuration, bounded leases and exact immutable remote-admission provenance. Check the live lease inside admission and before fresh dispatch; revocation does not prove upstream cancellation. Never plan or resend admitted model work through recovery. Preserve the shared USD 10 UTC-calendar-month search ceiling and USD 0.10 investigation lifetime ceiling across providers, concurrency and restart. Reserve a reviewed maximum charge before paid search and retain unknown-charge holds until named-operator reconciliation. The internal ledger alone does not establish an integrated paid-transport guarantee or source rights; no paid provider is seeded. Model inference and subscription fees remain separate.

## No-go changes without a new ADR and user review

Collapsing the three truth stores; automatic thesis edits; automatic canonical-edge promotion; general web search as the watcher; permanent multi-agent swarm; online model training; MVP Discovery; broker execution; production dependence on unreviewed data rights; or a dedicated graph DB selected without evidence.

## Definition of a handoff-ready increment

The increment identifies its source and domain contracts, updates relevant docs/ADRs, shows an end-to-end trace, verifies authority and replay invariants, reports costs and failure behavior, and lists any unresolved product or licensing question. Do not claim the full v1 universe is supported until coverage tests pass per pack.
