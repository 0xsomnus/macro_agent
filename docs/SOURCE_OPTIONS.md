# Initial continuous source options

Verified against public primary documentation on 2026-10-06. This is a proposal for review, not an accepted provider or permission to process content. No account was created, subscription purchased, key used or provider contacted. Access and latency were not measured.

The first live desk needs unexpected global developments as well as official releases. Coverage must extend beyond asset tickers: a supply disruption or overseas policy change may matter to ES, NQ, XAU, the dollar and FX without mentioning them. A scheduled-release connector alone cannot pass the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) gate.

## Proposed order

### Narrow official-source recheck, 2026-10-09

The [Fed press feed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/feeds/press_all.xml) returned HTTP 200 with 20 RSS items in a bounded read-only probe. Items carried headline, link, GUID, description, category and publication claim; the first two descriptions repeated the headlines. The [official directory](https://www.federalreserve.gov/feeds/feeds.htm) describes links, headlines and brief summaries. This is headline/summary input, with no archive-completeness or latency proof. The existing adapter's `truncated=False` only describes its local parser bound, not publisher coverage.

BLS officially lists [release feeds](https://www.bls.gov/feed/) and an [Eastern Time release calendar](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/). Direct CPI, Employment Situation and calendar endpoint probes returned HTTP 403 from this environment. Public documentation was accessible, but usable acquisition and actual payload compatibility remain unverified. A schedule is planned timing, not publication or receipt evidence.

The [Fed policy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm) and [BLS copyright policy](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm) still support the narrow first-party text proposal with attribution and their stated exceptions. Exclude third-party material, copyrighted photographs/illustrations, and protected seals, logos and emblems. Neither policy specifically discusses inference routers. Linked pages require their own scope review. No source was activated, linked article fetched, account created or paid call made in this recheck.

### Broader feed evaluation

Compare a Newsquawk enterprise evaluation quote with a Benzinga API evaluation. Ask for a small internal application licence before purchasing a retail terminal subscription. Test the same global macro examples on both, including an off-map severe disruption, a correction and gradual offsetting developments. Provider editorial selection may omit developments relevant to a particular thesis; it is an upstream recall dependency.

While that choice remains open, recorded receipts can prove daemon recovery and an official-source connector can prove a limited permitted acquisition path. GDELT metadata is an optional context experiment. It cannot turn keyword search into the general watcher or authorize arbitrary article retrieval. None of these interim paths should be called full continuous desk coverage.

## Required source-health contract

- Pin source contract, entitlement, endpoint/feed scope, native identity/revision and lawful payload hash. Redact credentials, including tokens in query strings.
- Preserve source-claimed publication time, actual receipt time and durable admission evidence separately. Unknown public availability remains unknown. Backfilled material cannot become known at an earlier replay cutoff.
- Record last successful check, receipt, heartbeat when available, retry/backoff, lag, parse failures, rights expiry and missing intervals. A quiet feed is not automatically unavailable; a successful connection is not proof of fresh or complete data.
- Advance capture checkpoints only with durable accepted receipts. Preserve corrections and removals; neither an in-place overwrite nor replaying only the latest story is sufficient.
- Detect pagination, result caps and incomplete catch-up. Benzinga's documented WebSocket replay covers only the last 100 cached messages, so reconnect needs a separately validated recovery path. GDELT DOC saturation must be exposed, not treated as a complete window. [Stream recovery](https://docs.benzinga.com/ws-reference/data-websocket/get-news-stream), [DOC limits](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/)
- Keep capture and health checks independent of model keys, analysis budgets and relevance classifiers. Unknown relevance remains unresolved. Degraded source coverage must appear alongside any brief.

## Questions for source selection

1. What monthly acquisition budget is acceptable, separately from inference, and would a quote-based macro feed be preferable to a cheaper feed with narrower verified coverage?
2. For internal research, what detection delay is acceptable, and what stricter delay would the paper pilot require for a credible severe surprise?
3. Can the provider's written evaluation cover retained revisions, remote model processing through our selected router, derived thesis briefs and a small named set of testers, with explicit transition terms for a later commercial pilot?

The unresolved contract checklist is API availability/hours, completeness and exclusions, publication/correction semantics, replay retention, quotas, failure handling, attribution, third-party processing, raw/excerpt retention, derived-output display, shared reuse, tester scope, termination/deletion and all associated fees. Capture mechanics and analytical usefulness are evaluated separately, per [monitoring contracts](MONITORING_AND_SOURCES.md) and [ADR 013](ADR/013-internal-byok-and-cost-boundaries.md).
