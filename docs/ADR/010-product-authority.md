# ADR 010: Product authority and regulatory perimeter

Status: Accepted product boundary; legal classification open.

Updated 2026-10-02. Continuous desk function and interpretation authority clarified.

**Decision:** The agent acts as a continuous research and monitoring desk for a solo/retail discretionary macro or fundamental trader: morning briefs, portfolio-aware event analysis, counter-analysis, and collaborative thesis refinement. It offers conditional hypotheses, evidence, uncertainty, and signposts. User judgment and execution remain final; no autonomous trading or broker controls. Personalized hedge or trade recommendations need product and jurisdiction-specific legal review before release.

**Why:** Experienced and newer traders need rigorous challenge without substituted authority. A half-formed thesis can become more supported, explicit, and falsifiable; this does not imply greater conviction or a bullish conclusion. UI and service permissions must reflect that promise. A disclaimer alone does not resolve regulatory duties.

**Rejected:** A prescriptive AI trader, one-click orders, or treating “not financial advice” text as sufficient compliance.

**Consequence:** User approval activates both exact thesis text and concise interpretation. Material interpretation changes need renewed approval; extra investigated drivers remain agent hypotheses. Factual error, unsupported mechanism, missing detail, and defensible disagreement receive different responses. Audit, actual source support, coverage disclosure, source rights, feature flags, marketing review, and written specialist counsel remain launch gates where applicable. An urgent qualified notice can precede resolved portfolio impact, but cannot assert unsupported consequences.
