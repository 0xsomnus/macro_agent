# ADR 002: Continuous monitoring before expensive reasoning

Status: Accepted; clarified 2026-10-02. Supersedes the implication that all relevance can be settled deterministically before analysis. See [ADR 011](011-materiality-and-continuous-desk.md).

**Decision:** Compile versioned source manifests per approved thesis driver/exposure. Deterministic watchers capture scheduled changes and continuous permitted news independently of models. Broad screening may use bounded classification; possible relevance, investigation, and interruption are distinct decisions. Uncertainty or classification failure cannot mean low relevance. Search remains escalation for a named unresolved material question.

**Why:** Independent capture preserves timely awareness during model outages. Continuous news is essential to the solo-trader desk promise; calendars alone do not establish that capability. Semantic relevance requires qualified analysis in some cases, including surprises outside existing driver paths.

**Rejected:** General search as the watcher; novelty alone bypassing into alerts; silent drops on failed classification; a scheduled-release-only pilot.

**Consequence:** Validate permitted news and source timeliness/failure contracts as pilot gates. Coverage and source health are inspectable. Qualified urgent notices can precede completed analysis. Detection limits remain explicit, including missed or unobserved events.

**History:** Accepted 2026-09-28 with independent watchers and targeted search. Clarified after user review on 2026-10-02 to distinguish independent acquisition from model-assisted relevance and to make continuous news core.
