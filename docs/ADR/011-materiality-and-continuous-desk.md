# ADR 011: Materiality and the continuous discretionary trading desk

Status: Accepted 2026-10-02, following user review of audit findings 1, 4, 6, and 10.

**Problem:** A deterministic low/material split can miss unfamiliar or cumulative developments. Headline alerts and citations alone do not establish useful portfolio analysis. Retries, revisions, and unavailable models can also undermine timely trustworthy delivery.

**Decision:** Build a continuous research/monitoring desk with morning briefs, analysis, counter-analysis, and collaborative user-approved thesis revision. Independent source capture feeds broad screening. Possible relevance, bounded investigation, and interruption have separate thresholds. Thesis impact and attached-trade impact can each escalate. Uncertain relevance and classifier failure remain unresolved, not low.

Credible potential significance through a plausible exposure path, or severe broad disruption, can justify investigation outside existing graph links. Novelty alone cannot trigger escalation or notification. Distinct developments accumulate by driver/horizon with duplicates and offsetting evidence handled. Macro context is global despite the bounded pilot instrument list.

Credible urgent unresolved developments can produce early qualified notices. One evolving event/thesis brief covers attached trades and receives later analysis/corrections; only material updates interrupt again. Cumulative multi-event briefs retain contributing evidence. Fact support is checked against exact passages/fields; portfolio effects remain qualified hypotheses. Missing expectations block surprise/priced-in claims. Unsupported consequences narrow the notice rather than erase the established event.

Persist assessment, brief revision, and delivery intent atomically. Idempotent leased retries, acknowledgements, and visible terminal failures protect delivery. Revisions supersede stale dependent analysis; concurrent outdated completions cannot become current. Channel limitations preclude a universal exactly-once promise.

**Why:** The product earns attention by connecting evidence to user exposure and explaining changes, while preserving timely awareness and explicit uncertainty. Sensitivity to surprises must coexist with bounded spend and noise control.

**Rejected:** Novelty-only bypasses, calendar-only monitoring, treating failure as irrelevance, headline count as evidence accumulation, forcing unsupported conclusions, silent alert loss, and implying comprehensive global coverage.

**Consequence:** Evaluate independent misses, unsupported claims, latency/noise/cost, and useful portfolio connections against a simpler same-source desk. Source and dated coverage contracts are required. Threshold values and accumulation windows remain explicit empirical decisions, not settled product constants. This ADR changes no user-thesis or canonical-knowledge approval authority.
