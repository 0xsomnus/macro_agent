# ADR 008: Point-in-time inputs; event studies and regulatory workflows deferred

Status: Accepted principles; implementation sequencing revised 2026-10-02. Statistical event studies and specialised regulatory lifecycle workflows are deferred from the initial pilot.

**Decision:** Collect permitted timestamped event/expectation/market inputs prospectively. Distinguish public availability, actual system receipt, publication, and revision known-at time. Preserve exact assessment dependency versions. Historical data requires verifiable original vintage and cannot backfill operational knowledge. When later implemented, event studies start with FOMC, CPI, and NFP. Regulatory process state advances from official actions, with interpretation separate.

**Why:** Surprise and priced-in claims require valid expectations. Historical replay must separate what was public from what the desk received. Models may know historical outcomes, so replay does not establish foresight. Lifecycle state and effective dates cannot be inferred from headline wording alone.

**Rejected:** Statistical studies as a launch prerequisite; unvintaged backfills in operational replay; price reaction alone as evidence of expectations or causality; treating a wire as a canonical legislative action.

**Consequence:** The pilot can analyse facts/mechanisms/reactions with expectation limits disclosed and retain basic outcome records. Later studies require data feasibility and evaluation gates. Define but do not schedule the dedicated regulatory graph; relevant policy news still enters continuous contextual monitoring. Additional families/jurisdictions need reviewed mappings; congressional-trading monitoring remains deferred pending legal/data-rights review.

**History:** Accepted 2026-09-28 with narrow studies and stateful regulation. Revised after user review on 2026-10-02 to prioritise the continuous desk pilot, preserve prospective data collection, and defer specialised machinery.
