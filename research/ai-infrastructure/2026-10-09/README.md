Research cutoff: 2026-10-09. Intended holding period: a few weeks to a few months.

The AI buildout has observable revenue, order and electricity-demand support. That does not establish attractive prospective returns at current prices. The user's question remains a research question, not an approved thesis or an instruction to transact.

These are research candidates. Company examples identify exposures, not recommendations or certified product coverage.

## Is it too late?

**Unresolved.** The defensible conclusion is narrower: there is a real buildout to investigate, but a trade needs an asset-specific change relative to expectations within the chosen horizon.

1. Separate demand growth, supplier profit and returns to the actual instrument holder. Each requires another causal link.
2. Compare current price with plausible growth, margins, reinvestment and dilution. Do not annualize an exceptional quarter as normalized earnings.
3. Identify a dated catalyst and what would constitute a positive or negative surprise. Company guidance is management's forecast, not market consensus.
4. Include contract basis, funding or CFD carry and execution assumptions when evaluating paper results.

The SOXX issuer snapshot shows a large year-to-date gain and a substantial reported portfolio P/E. This establishes that prices have moved, not that AMD or NVIDIA is overvalued, or that the buildout is fully priced in. No current company-specific reverse valuation, verified consensus vintage or complete copper curve/inventory assessment was produced here. [Snapshot and limitations](EVIDENCE.md#prices-expectations-and-near-term-catalysts)

The copper evidence is particularly useful counter-analysis: a conditional 2035 mine-supply gap can coexist with forecast refined-metal surpluses in 2026 and 2027. Those measures and horizons must not be merged into a near-term shortage claim. [Copper sources](EVIDENCE.md#operating-demand-and-supply-response)
